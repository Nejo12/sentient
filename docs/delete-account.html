Delete your account

Signed-in users can start permanent account deletion directly inside Sentient.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Sentient and select the You tab.
  2. Under Account, confirm that you are signed in to the account you intend to delete.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

What is deleted

The deletion request removes the Sentient authentication account and associated persisted Sentient data, including saved rewrite history and account-scoped settings held by Sentient. Data that must be retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations may be retained only for the required period.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting a Sentient account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel recurring billing separately in your store subscription settings.

Deletion is permanent

Account deletion cannot be undone. A later return to Sentient requires creating a new account, and deleted rewrite history cannot be restored.

Cannot access the app?

Contact larneygee@gmail.com from the email address associated with the account. Do not include passwords, private messages, access tokens or payment details. Additional verification may be required before an external deletion request can be processed.
