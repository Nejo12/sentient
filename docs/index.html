Sentient

Say it well, before you send.

Sentient helps you fix a reply before you send it, without leaving WhatsApp, Messenger, or iMessage. Share a message, pick how you want to be understood, and choose the reply that sounds like you.

Share

Select a message in any chat app and share it to Sentient, or use the floating bubble on Android.

Choose

Tell Sentient what you need — help saying something well, or help seeing what you might be missing.

Compare and send

Pick from a few ways to say it, edit if you like, then copy it back into your conversation. Nothing sends on its own.

Available for iOS, with an Android floating bubble as the second entry point.
