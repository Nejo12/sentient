Sentient

Say it well, before you send.

Sentient helps you understand ambiguous messages, separate evidence from assumptions and prepare a reply that communicates your intent clearly. It only works with text you deliberately paste or share, and it never sends a message automatically.

Provide the message

Paste text into Sentient or use a supported system Share Sheet. If WhatsApp does not expose Share, copy and paste remains the reliable fallback.

Understand before replying

Review plausible interpretations, what cannot be known from the words alone and communication risks worth considering.

Choose and send yourself

Start with one recommended reply, edit it when needed, then copy it back to the conversation. Nothing sends on its own.

Available for iOS and Android. Android also offers an optional floating bubble that opens Sentient after you copy text; it does not monitor chats.
