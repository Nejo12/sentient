Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Sentient helps people understand ambiguous messages and prepare clearer replies. This policy explains what information Sentient handles and why. Sentient only works with text you deliberately paste or share. It does not monitor your conversations in the background.

What Sentient receives

Sentient receives message text only when you explicitly paste it, share it through a supported system Share Sheet, or open the optional Android floating bubble after copying text. The bubble does not read chats directly and Sentient does not use an Android Accessibility Service to read other applications. Optional details such as a rough draft, contact label, source-app label and chosen communication style may be included when you submit a request.

How submitted text is processed

Submitted text is sent over an encrypted connection to a server-side service built with Supabase Edge Functions. That service calls OpenAI's API to generate communication analysis and reply guidance. API credentials are stored server-side and are not embedded in the mobile app. Sentient does not use submitted messages for advertising or tracking.

Your account and rewrite history

Signing in is optional for the core flow. If you sign in and history saving is enabled, Sentient can store submitted messages, selected replies, optional labels and timestamps in Supabase so your rewrite history can be available on your account. Row-level access controls are used to isolate account data. You can disable future history saving from Settings.

Subscriptions

Sentient Pro purchases are processed by Apple or Google and entitlement status is managed using RevenueCat. Sentient does not receive or store your full card or bank details. Store and RevenueCat identifiers may be processed to determine whether Pro access is active.

What Sentient does not do

  • It does not monitor chats, notifications or typing in the background.
  • It does not use an Accessibility Service to read content from other apps.
  • It does not send replies automatically.
  • It does not sell personal data or use submitted messages for targeted advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account directly in the app from Settings → Account → Delete account. This initiates deletion of the authentication account and associated persisted Sentient data. Step-by-step guidance is available on the account deletion page. Privacy or deletion questions may also be sent to the contact address below.

Third-party processors

  • OpenAI — communication analysis and reply generation.
  • Supabase — authentication, database storage and server-side functions.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchase processing and store services.

Security

Sentient uses encrypted network transport and keeps provider credentials server-side. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so access is limited to the data needed to provide the requested feature and users remain in control of whether text is submitted or saved.

Children

Sentient is not intended to collect personal data from children below the minimum age required to consent to digital services in their country. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal data should contact us.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. The policy published here should be reviewed together with the production app and its current service providers.
