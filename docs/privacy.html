Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Sentient helps you fix a reply before you send it. This policy explains what information Sentient handles, and why. Sentient is built to only read a message when you deliberately share it — it never reads your chats in the background.

What Sentient reads

Sentient only receives a message when you explicitly share it to the app (via the iOS share sheet or the Android floating bubble), or when you type a rough draft yourself. It does not have access to your chat apps, contacts, or messages beyond what you share.

How your message is processed

The message you share, together with any rough draft and the tone or intent you choose, is sent to our server-side rewriting service (built on Supabase Edge Functions), which calls OpenAI's API to generate rewrite options. Your message is not stored by OpenAI beyond what is required to generate a response, and our server never stores your OpenAI API credentials on your device because there are none on your device — the key is kept server-side only.

Your account and history

If you sign in, Sentient can save your rewrite history (the messages you've shared and the replies you chose) to your account via Supabase, so you can find them again later. This is private to your account. You can turn history saving off at any time in Settings — existing entries are not created while it's off. Signing in is optional; the core rewriting flow works without an account, subject to the free daily limit.

Subscriptions

Sentient Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store (and, on Android, Google Play) and managed through RevenueCat, which tells our app whether your subscription is active. We do not process or store your payment details ourselves — that is handled entirely by Apple/Google.

What Sentient never does

  • It never reads or watches your chats in the background.
  • It never sends a reply on your behalf — you always copy and send it yourself.
  • It never sells your data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

If you have an account, you can request deletion of your account and associated history at any time by contacting us (see below). We will remove your stored data within a reasonable time of your request.

Third-party services used

  • OpenAI — generates the rewrite options and advice, called server-side only.
  • Supabase — authentication, rewrite history storage, and the server-side function that talks to OpenAI.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and entitlement management.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
