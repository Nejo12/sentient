# Sentient 1.0 store listing

## Product position

Sentient helps people understand ambiguous messages, identify uncertainty and prepare a clear reply without pretending to know another person's intent.

## Apple App Store

**Name**  
Sentient

**Subtitle**  
Understand before replying

**Promotional text**  
See possible meanings, understand what remains uncertain and prepare a reply that communicates your intent clearly.

**Description**

Sentient is communication intelligence for conversations that matter.

Share or paste a message and Sentient helps you:

- see more than one plausible interpretation
- separate evidence from assumptions
- identify uncertainty and communication risks
- get one recommended reply first
- open deeper reasoning and alternative replies only when needed
- keep saved rewrites private to your account

Sentient does not monitor your conversations and does not send messages automatically. It only analyses text you deliberately paste or share.

## Google Play

**App name**  
Sentient

**Short description**  
Understand messages clearly and prepare thoughtful replies.

**Full description**

Sentient helps you pause, understand and reply clearly.

Paste or share a message to explore plausible meanings, see what cannot be known from the words alone and prepare a response that matches how you want to be understood.

Start with one recommended reply. Open deeper interpretation, uncertainty, risks and alternatives only when you need them.

Sentient only processes text you deliberately provide. It does not read chats in the background and never sends a message without your action.
