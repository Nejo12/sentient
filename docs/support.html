Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us if you need help.

How does Sentient work?

Share a message from any chat app, or use the floating bubble on Android. Sentient gives you a few ways to say it back — you pick the one that sounds like you, edit it if you like, then copy it back into your conversation. Nothing sends automatically.

Does Sentient read my messages in the background?

No. Sentient only reads a message when you share it. It never watches your chats in the background, and history is private to your account and can be turned off in Settings.

How do I cancel Sentient Pro?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions on iOS), the same as any other subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

Something isn't working — what do I do?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the problem continues, get in touch using the email below and describe what happened, including your device and iOS version if you can.
