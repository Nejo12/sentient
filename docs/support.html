Support

Help with sharing messages, account access, subscriptions and technical problems.

Share or paste a message

Where a chat app exposes the system Share option, select the message, tap Share and choose Sentient. Some WhatsApp contexts do not expose Share. In that case, copy the message, open Sentient and paste it. On Android, the optional floating bubble can open Sentient after you copy text; it does not read chats automatically.

Sentient is missing from the iOS Share Sheet

  1. Open a shareable piece of text and tap Share.
  2. Scroll through the app row and tap More.
  3. Enable Sentient and optionally add it to favourites.
  4. If it still does not appear, restart the device and try again from Notes or Safari.

Android floating bubble

The bubble is optional and requires permission to draw over other apps. Enable it only from Sentient's setup screen. You can revoke the permission later in Android system settings. If the bubble stops appearing, reopen Sentient and confirm that the overlay permission is still active.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Use the restore-purchases option in the subscription flow when an active purchase is not recognised. Subscription cancellation and billing changes are managed through your store account.

Account and data

You can delete a signed-in account from Settings in the app. See the account deletion guide for exact steps. Read the privacy policy for information about data processing.

Contact support

Email larneygee@gmail.com. Include the platform, app version, a brief description of the problem and the steps that caused it. Do not send private message content, passwords, access tokens or payment details.
